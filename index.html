How it works

From time in
to time out.

A straightforward flow for your staff. A smoother experience for every guest.

01
STEP 01

Check In & Start Timer

Cashiers select an available room, table, or court on the interactive floor map. Choose between hourly rates, flat packages, or VIP membership pricing.

  • Instant live clock synchronization
  • Preset grace periods & minimum sessions
  • Auto-generates digital customer tab

Check In & Start Timer

Select an open table or room, assign rate tiers or promo packages, and trigger the live timer instantly.

02
STEP 02

Add Food, Drinks & Cues

Attach bar snacks, beverages, cue rentals, or paddles directly to the room's running tab without opening separate transactions.

  • Real-time kitchen / bar inventory deduction
  • One-tap items for quick counter orders
  • Consolidated orders under one tab

Add Food, Drinks & Cues

Charge kitchen orders, bar drinks, or equipment rentals directly to the active table tab with one click.

03
STEP 03

Live Monitoring & Alerts

View every table status from the cashier terminal. Flashing badges and gentle chime alerts trigger as time expires or when incoming reservations are due.

  • Visual countdowns & overtime tracking
  • Prevents double-booking clashes
  • Pause timer support for temporary breaks

Live Monitoring & Alerts

Track live run times from the cashier desk with automatic notifications before reserved slots begin.

04
STEP 04

Unified Fast Checkout

End sessions cleanly. System automatically computes exact pro-rated minutes plus added food and drinks into a single itemized receipt.

  • Split bills between players easily
  • Accepts cash, cards, and GCash/Maya
  • Instant thermal print & e-receipts

Unified Fast Checkout

Stop the session and print or email an itemized bill consolidating rental minutes, discounts, and orders.

05
STEP 05

Shift Close & Reports

Reconcile cash drawers at end of shift with automated discrepancy detection, sales summaries, and cloud-backed audit records.

  • Float and drawer variance audit
  • Revenue breakdown by hourly rental vs. POS
  • Owner summaries sent via email

Shift Close & Reports

Reconcile cash drawers, review revenue summaries, and monitor peak hours with automated end-of-day reports.
